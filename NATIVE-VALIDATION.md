# iPhone release validation

The release gates are an Xcode build, a signed iPhone installation with all three extensions, and paired testing with a desktop build that implements the secure transport and receipt contract. Record the phone and desktop commit IDs, iOS version, signing configuration, and result for each device run. [Secure companion protocol](SECURE-COMPANION.md)

## Checks completed in the Linux workspace

- `bash scripts/test-settings.sh` passed the portable preference, cancellation, input acknowledgement, Bluetooth keypad decoding, history, received-file storage, transfer staging, Share receipt, incoming receipt, and Widget tests.
- Swift parsing with the `arm64-apple-ios17.0` target passed for the app, all three extensions, and shared source. Apple SDK type checking requires Xcode.
- `actionlint` 1.7.12 and `git diff --check` passed.
- The sanitized Linux desktop build passed all 90 tests, including the tagged incoming receipt exchange, interrupted pairing, and the screen stream idle deadline.

## Build and signing

1. Run the **iPhone app** workflow with its default manual settings or as a normal PR/branch build. Verify that Xcode 26.5 builds the app and all three extensions, the IPA contains all three `.appex` bundles and the privacy manifests, and the run produces an artifact. A version tag or explicitly opted-in preview must point to the built commit.
2. Generate the project with XcodeGen. Use the same signing team and two distinct registered App Groups. Verify that the signed app has both groups, Broadcast and Share share only `PORTHOLE_APP_GROUP`, and Widgets has only `PORTHOLE_WIDGET_APP_GROUP`. Provision each target with its corresponding groups. If the app bundle ID changes, verify that the broadcast extension ID is the app ID plus `.broadcast` and that the iOS picker selects it. [Apple app group configuration](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/configuring-app-groups)
3. With a paired PC, switch to another app and restart Porthole on the PC. Verify the companion reconnects and can receive a file without reopening the iPhone app. Repeat during screen sharing with **Include app audio** enabled and **iPhone Speaker** selected. Open **Audio output** on the sharing screen and in Settings. Select Porthole to verify PC-only playback during the broadcast, then return to **iPhone Speaker** and verify broadcast audio resumes. Check that background playback stops when the PC disconnects deliberately or the user forgets the pairing.
4. With iLoader, install the iLoader artifact and keep all three extensions. Open Porthole and add its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Confirm the widgets see the same snapshot after the app opens and after a file arrives. Verify Broadcast and Share can still connect.

## Paired phone and PC

1. **Setup and trust.** On a fresh install, confirm the app waits for a connection action before discovery. Test secure QR pairing and manual pairing with the copied address, displayed code and SHA-256 fingerprint. Verify that Find nearby PCs lists a PC on the same Wi-Fi and that a saved pairing reconnects after the PC's DHCP address changes. Verify the saved identity reconnects. A different certificate, stale pairing code, and untrusted nearby entry must fail pairing. Check that Forget PC disconnects and a second PC requires verification. Confirm the only destination in Share → Porthole is the current verified PC and that changing the PC invalidates an open transfer review.
2. **Channel authorization.** Verify TLS 1.3 and the expected desktop certificate on 47731, 47732 and 47733. Confirm `AUTH` is checked before enabling screen or inbox traffic. Revoking or replacing the active companion session must stop its associated streams. Check that a signed extension with the proper App Group can access the active pairing, while an installation with a mismatched entitlement reports that shared pairing is unavailable.
3. **Broadcast and input.** On iOS 26, verify light/dark appearance, native tabs, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and the real system broadcast confirmation. Check connecting, live, waiting for screen changes, reconnecting, and the Stop sharing confirmation. Disconnect Wi-Fi, restore the same pairing, and confirm renewed stream authorization. Return Home during an active broadcast and confirm the PC still receives frames. Confirm Stop sharing ends the iOS broadcast and the status indicator clears. Repeat while AirPlay or local recording is active and verify the UI shows Porthole as live only during its own stream. Test input preferences across pairing, broadcast stop, a disconnect, and relaunch. The desktop should echo the current `INPUT` revision and values after applying them. Check the earlier iOS fallback controls on a supported device as available.
   Pair Bluetooth, open **Mouse and keyboard → Verify Bluetooth**, and confirm verification completes before companion controls activate. Verify again with a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi interruption, on dismissal and backgrounding, and with VoiceOver enabled. With the default policy, confirm controls activate only during Porthole screen sharing. Turn off **Only during screen sharing** and verify deliberate use without a broadcast. Test that switching the PC’s active Bluetooth connection to another device clears permission.
4. **Clipboard sync and history.** Check `SYNC` revision acknowledgements and rapid disable/re-enable with a paste callback pending. Test multiline text, emoji, and an image after the current setting is acknowledged. Confirm pasteboard writes are `localOnly`. On a fresh install, shared content should appear in Activity only after enabling **Keep clipboard history**. Verify the seven-day, 100-item and 16 MiB retention limits. Copy retained text and images while disconnected, confirm Copy stays local, and use Send to PC separately. Turn off history and verify that persisted content is cleared across relaunch. Confirm old summary-only entries indicate when full content is unavailable.
5. **Incoming storage and receipts.** Receive files into the default local folder, a selected On My iPhone folder, and iCloud Drive. Test a 512 MiB file and an over-limit file. Interrupt before the body completes, during storage, and after commit but before receipt. Verify the desktop and phone use the tagged `PUSH2` or `PUSH3` with `OFFSET`, `PENDING`, `SAVED`, and `NO`, that a retry with the same transfer identity writes one file, and that the desktop treats `SAVED` as its durable receipt. Record incoming header and receipt compatibility against the desktop build under test.
6. **Folder and history behavior.** Change the save folder and reopen older entries. Revoke provider access and review the reported local fallback for new files. Send distinct transfers with identical filenames and verify neither document is overwritten. Clear Received and Sent history and confirm the actual files stay in Files. Open a received file, preview it, export or share a copy, close the sheet, and reopen it. Test cleanup while a nested exporter still holds its preview.
7. **Notification permission and routing.** Check the one-time iOS prompt after pairing and the **File notifications** toggle. Test granting and denying permission, Focus, and disabling alerts in the app. Verify one in-app arrival banner, an Activity badge for unread files, and file opening from authorized system notifications while backgrounded or after cold launch. Tap a notification while a different sheet is open and test opening after clearing history. System delivery depends on iOS settings and the file must still be accessible.
8. **Direct and Share sends.** Review the picked files, normalized filenames, and verified destination. Test cancel during preparation and transfer, Wi-Fi loss, re-pairing, and partial batches. With `PSHR 2` and `PSHR 3`, verify retries reuse transfer IDs and the PC saves each file once, including when a receipt is lost. Close the Share extension or terminate the app mid-transfer, then reopen the pending batch in Connect to verify the saved offset and retained review. With `PSHR 1`, verify an indeterminate save state asks for review on the PC before sending again. Compare the PC destination, its durable receipt and the iPhone Sent entry. Confirm a Share receipt appears in Activity after the main app opens.
9. **Photos originals.** Test denied, limited, and granted photo access, an iCloud-only item, a unique match, multiple candidates, an edited asset, and explicit original selection. Confirm each matched original requires approval and identifies the destination. Cancel on the PC during matching and confirm the phone closes the review. Verify original bytes and expected metadata up to 512 MiB, clean rejection above the limit, and `FILE2`/`RECEIVED` or `REJECTED` matching the actual PC save result. Verify the phone records Sent only when the PC acknowledges the final save.
10. **Camera, rotation, and preview.** Dismiss the scanner during the permission prompt, scan an invalid QR and then a valid one, and repeat after disabling camera access. Rotate during ReplayKit, interrupt capture, then restart and verify the video orientation and fresh keyframes. Open PDF, image, and video previews with VoiceOver and at the largest Dynamic Type size.
11. **Large and interrupted workloads.** Review 500 selected files and verify Send and Cancel remain reachable. Test low disk space, slow or revoked provider access, a dropped screen frame acknowledgement, and a large Photos library with limited access changed during indexing. Record peak memory and test sustained transfer and capture on a supported physical iPhone.

## Widgets

1. Add Porthole's small and medium Home Screen widgets and the three Lock Screen placements. Check empty, caught-up, receiving, interrupted, save failure, new files and PC disconnected states. Verify light, dark, and tinted modes and test the medium shortcut hit regions and VoiceOver labels on a compact iPhone.
2. Receive one file, then several. Check that counts update after durable saves, including fallback to the local folder. Open one file from a notification while Received sits behind the preview and let another file arrive. Verify the new arrival stays unread until the Received list is unobscured. Open Received from a widget and confirm the count clears. Dismissing an alert retains unread files. Clear Received history and verify documents remain in Files. Relaunch the app to verify unread state persists.
3. Tap each shortcut during a cold launch, while a sheet is open, and when the PC is offline. Verify Share screen and Send files resume the requested destination if the remembered PC reconnects in time. Confirm both preserve their normal system confirmation or file review. Clipboard opens its Activity tab without reading the pasteboard or marking Received items read.
4. Enable iOS widget redaction and check that counts and state icons are hidden on the Lock Screen and in StandBy. Confirm file notifications remain generic and still open the correct file.

## Recovery and privacy regressions

- Interrupt a new pairing before the PC saves the key, after the PC saves the key, and while the phone restarts. Confirm the code or staged key recovers the same pairing as appropriate. Check that the connected state appears after commitment and that pairing secrets and the QR camera preview stay covered during active capture.
- Pause or interrupt frame delivery and check that Live clears when frame acknowledgements stop. Tap Stop sharing while Wi-Fi is down, then restore Wi-Fi and verify delivery stays stopped.
- Test a large approved Photos original while changing limited-library access or disabling clipboard or input controls. Confirm the stream stops promptly and unfinished files stay out of Sent. Try an unavailable video match alongside an available one. Open Received or Clipboard from a cold widget launch with a large authorized Photos library and verify Photos indexing waits for Photo access or a PC request.

The signed device and paired-desktop checks are pending. Treat incoming receipt alignment, signing and App Group setup, and background-resume behavior as release gates until tested with the matching builds.
